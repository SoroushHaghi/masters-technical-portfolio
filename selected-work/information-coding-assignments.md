# Information & Coding Theory — assessed analytical assignments

**Domain:** [Communication, Information & Coding Theory](../domains/communication-information-coding.md)  
**Type:** Named, graded university written coursework.

## Work represented

Personally associated written problem sets in information and coding theory, including source coding, prefix/uniquely decodable codes, Kraft–McMillan inequalities and related mathematical reasoning.

## Deliverable

Submitted analytical homework with instructor markings. These records document participation and problem-solving practice, **not** blanket correctness: some submissions include partial scores and corrections.

## Public artifact status

Original graded PDFs contain private educational information and may have errors. They are not published. Selected calculations can be independently checked, reworked and written as clean original examples for future public demonstration.
