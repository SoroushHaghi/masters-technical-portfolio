# Nano-Optics journal club — Sn-nanobar plasmonic metasurfaces

**[View original presentation slides (PDF)](../assets/presentations/Nanooptics%20Presentation%20TU%20Braunschweig%20Haghi.pdf)**

**Domain:** [Quantum Devices & Physical Systems](../domains/quantum-devices-physical-systems.md)  
**Type:** **Literature-review presentation** of published research.

## What I worked on

- Studied a published hexagonally arranged tin-nanobar metasurface and its plasmonic resonances.
- Explained the published Finite-Difference Time-Domain (FDTD) setup and how nanobar length, orientation and incident polarization affect resonance wavelengths and field patterns.
- Interpreted the reported resonance trends, including approximately 0.82 µm and 2.38 µm peaks in the discussed publication.

## Deliverable

A journal-club presentation explaining the published optical model and scientific results.
