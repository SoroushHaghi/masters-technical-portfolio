## What I worked on

- Studied and prepared a presentation on **state-dependent communication channels** and the distinction between source and channel coding.
- Reviewed **random-binning** and encoder-side state-information concepts, including Gelfand–Pinsker / dirty-paper-coding arguments and achievable-rate reasoning at an academic level.
- Organized and explained these results in a technical presentation.

## Deliverable

A course presentation with conceptual and mathematical discussion of channel coding with state information.
