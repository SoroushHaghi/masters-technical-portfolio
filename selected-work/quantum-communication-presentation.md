## What I worked on

- Explained qubit states using the Bloch sphere, density operators, mixed-state purity and Pauli operators.
- Presented **quantum-state tomography as a concept**, including measurements in different bases and Bloch-vector reconstruction.
- Compared classical and entanglement-assisted strategies through the **CHSH game**, and discussed quantum entanglement, dense coding and teleportation.
- Connected these concepts to multiple-access communication models and qualitative interpretations of quantum advantage.
